PagePocket is your private Wayback Machine. Save any web page as a complete, high-fidelity copy - HTML, CSS, images, and fonts - and keep it in your own private cloud library, searchable and shareable.

Websites change, go offline, or disappear. PagePocket makes sure the page you found is the page you keep, permanently. It is built for professionals who need reliable records of the web: researchers, journalists, developers, and archivists.

---

Key Features

Full-Fidelity Page Saving
PagePocket captures the complete page as it appeared:

- HTML content - the page structure and all its text
- Images - photos, graphics, and background images
- Stylesheets (CSS) - the exact visual design
- Fonts - embedded webfonts so archived pages render correctly

Private Cloud Library
Your archives sync to your private PagePocket library:

- Free plan: 50 pages per rolling 30 days, 500 MB of storage
- Pro: $2/month (billed yearly at $24/year), effectively unlimited pages, 10 GB of storage
- Your library is private - nothing is public unless you share it

Full-Text Search
Search across everything you have ever saved. Find a page again by any word it contained, not just by its title.

Nested Collections
Organize saved pages into nested collections with colors, so large research projects stay structured and easy to browse.

Clean Reader View
Open any saved page in a clean, readable view. The reader shows a static snapshot of the page: scripts never run inside archived pages. That is deliberate - it keeps every archive faithful to what you captured and safe to open.

Share Links
Share any saved page with a private link:

- Optional expiry date on every link
- Revoke access at any time
- View counts show you when a link has been opened

Local ZIP Export
Download any archive as a ZIP file to keep your own offline copy - your data is never locked in.

How It Works

1. Click the PagePocket icon on any webpage you want to save
2. The page is saved in full fidelity to your private PagePocket library
3. Organize it into collections and find it again later with full-text search
4. Open it in the clean reader view, export it as a ZIP, or share it with a link

Install PagePocket from the Chrome Web Store:
https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/web-page-downloader/aeojmgngnebhbjpncamiplkimkbnmpmk

---

NB! Some websites go to great lengths to prevent anyone from downloading their content, and therefore for some websites the saved page may differ from the original. The reader view always shows a static snapshot - scripts never run inside archived pages.
