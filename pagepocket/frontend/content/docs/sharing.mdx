Your library is private by default — nothing is visible to anyone else until
you share it. When you do, a share link serves the archived copy itself, so
the recipient sees exactly what you saved, not whatever the live page says
today.

## Creating a link

Open a saved page and choose **Share**. Pick an expiry, then create the link:

- **Never** — the link works until you revoke it.
- **24 hours**, **7 days**, or **30 days** — the link stops working
  automatically after that window.
- **Custom date** — pick the exact day it should expire.

The full URL is shown **once, at creation** — copy it there. For security,
the link's secret is not stored in a form the app can re-reveal later; if you
lose it, simply create a new link. Your share dialog always shows whether a
link is active.

## What recipients see

Anyone with the link can open the archived page in their browser — **no
PagePocket account, no sign-up**. The page is the archived copy, rendered in
the same sandboxed viewer you use, under a "Shared via PagePocket" banner.
Its scripts never run, for you or for them.

Share links use unguessable tokens, and the link serves only that one page —
not your library, not your other saves.

## View counts and revoking

The share dialog shows how many **views** a link has had (repeated opens by
the same visitor are counted once), so you know whether a shared snapshot has
been seen.

**Revoke** removes access immediately — the link stops resolving, including
for people who saved it. Nothing is deleted from your library; revoking only
retires the link. Recreate one at any time.

## When a shared link dies on its own

A share link stops working when any of these happens:

- you revoked it,
- its expiry date passed, or
- **the saved page was deleted from your library.** A share is a window onto
  your archived copy — there is nothing to serve once the page is gone.

If you are handing someone a link that must keep working, pick **Never** as
the expiry and keep the page in your library.
