PagePocket saves full-fidelity copies of web pages to a private cloud library
you can search and share. This guide takes you from install to your first
saved page in a few minutes.

## 1. Install the extension

PagePocket ships as a Chrome extension. Install it from the
[Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/web-page-downloader/aeojmgngnebhbjpncamiplkimkbnmpmk)
by clicking **Add to Chrome**. After the first install the side panel opens
automatically; from then on you can open it from the extension's icon in the
toolbar.

The panel is where saves happen — it reads the page you have open and stages
the capture before anything is stored.

## 2. Create your account

You need an account to use the cloud library. You can create one in either
place — both are the same account:

- **On the website** at the [registration page](/register): email and
  password, no credit card.
- **In the extension**: open the side panel, tick **PagePocket Cloud
  Storage**, and use the **Register** tab.

The extension's sign-in and registration forms offer two more things:

- **Continue with Google** — appears when Google sign-in is enabled. It
  works for both signing in and registering, and honours the keep-signed-in
  choice below. If you already have a password account and use Google with
  the same email address, the two are linked into one account — no
  duplicate is created. No button? Google sign-in is not enabled yet;
  email and password always work.
- **Keep me signed in on this device** — ticked by default. Keep it ticked
  and you stay signed in when you close the browser: the extension stores
  a sign-in token on this device, rotates it automatically, and deletes it
  when you sign out. Untick it on shared computers to go back to being
  asked for your password after every browser restart.

The Free plan covers 50 page saves per rolling 30 days and 500 MB of storage,
and includes search, collections, and share links. You can upgrade later —
see [Pricing](/pricing) for the exact limits.

## 3. Save your first page

1. Open any web page you want to keep.
2. Open the PagePocket side panel.
3. Make sure **PagePocket Cloud Storage** is ticked and you are signed in.
4. Click **Start download**.

The panel walks through the capture for you — scraping the page, uploading
its resources, and assembling the copy in your library. When it reports
**Ready**, the page is in your library.

Two practical tips learned the hard way:

- **Keep the side panel open** and stay on the tab while the save runs.
  Closing the panel or navigating away can abort the capture.
- Pages that load content as you scroll are captured by scrolling the page
  automatically, but very long pages can take a little while.

## 4. Find it in your library

Open the [app](/app) and sign in — every saved page is there, listed with its
title, the original URL, and the time you saved it. From the library you can:

- open the archived copy in the viewer,
- organise pages into [collections](/docs/collections),
- [search the full text](/docs/full-text-search) of everything you have saved,
- [share any page](/docs/sharing) with a link.

## Download a saved page

Any page in your library can be downloaded as a ZIP:

1. Open the page in the viewer (or use the **⋯** menu on a dashboard card).
2. Click **Download** (or press `d` in the viewer).

You get a single `.zip` containing `index.html` and every asset, with the
same relative paths the viewer uses — unzip it anywhere and `index.html`
opens offline in your browser. Two things to know: the download is a
**static snapshot** (scripts never run in archived pages, so they are not
in the ZIP either), and downloading does not count against your monthly
save quota — it is a plain read of your own archive.
