Collections are folders for your library. They are how a pile of saved pages
becomes a usable archive: one collection per project, per story, per course,
per client — whatever structure fits the work.

## Creating a collection

In the [app](/app), use the sidebar's new-collection control and give it a
name. The collection appears immediately and can receive pages right away.

Collections are available on every plan, including Free.

## Nesting

Collections can contain sub-collections, so you can mirror real structure —
*Research* → *2026* → *Link-rot sources*, for example. There is no fixed
depth limit; nest as deep as the work demands. Expanding and collapsing the
tree in the sidebar keeps even large libraries navigable.

## Colors

Every collection has a color. Ten preset colors are available — assign one
from the collection's context menu and it is used consistently in the sidebar
and page lists, so a project is recognisable at a glance.

## Filing pages

Pages can be filed into collections two ways:

- **Drag and drop** a page onto a collection in the sidebar, or
- use a page's **move** action and pick the destination collection from the
  dialog — including creating a fresh collection on the way.

A page lives in one collection at a time, and the sidebar shows each
collection's page count so you can see where the mass of your archive sits.

## Renaming and deleting

Rename a collection from its context menu — links, shares, and saved pages
are unaffected. Deleting a collection keeps its pages: they return to the
unfiled view rather than being deleted with it, so tidying up is never
destructive.

## Finding things inside a collection

Open a collection in the sidebar to browse just its pages, or run a
[full-text search](/docs/full-text-search) and filter it to the collection —
useful for "which of the pages I saved for this story mentioned X?".
