The shift from a local downloader to a personal web archive:

- **Save straight to your cloud library** from the extension: sign in once
  and every capture lands in a private library you can open on any device.
- **Collections** to organise pages, **full-text search** across everything
  you have saved, and **share links** with optional expiry — the archive is
  now findable and shareable, not just stored.
- **Large saves became resilient:** progress checkpoints, session resume,
  keepalive during long uploads, and parallelised uploads and assembly make
  big pages faster to save and safe to resume after interruptions.
- The service moved to hardened production infrastructure in front of
  Cloudflare, with encrypted transport end to end.
