Signing in to PagePocket got two quality-of-life options:

- **Stay signed in on your own devices.** The sign-in and registration
  forms now include a pre-checked **Keep me signed in on this device**
  box. Leave it ticked and you stay signed in after closing the browser —
  the sign-in token stored on your device rotates automatically and is
  removed when you sign out. Untick it on shared computers and everything
  behaves as before: you sign in again after every browser restart.
- **Continue with Google.** When Google sign-in is enabled, a
  **Continue with Google** button appears on the extension's sign-in and
  registration forms. It works for both and respects the keep-signed-in
  choice. Using Google with the email address of an existing password
  account signs you into that same account — the two are linked
  automatically, so no duplicate account is created.

Email and password sign-in continues to work exactly as before, and the
same account works in the extension and on the website.
