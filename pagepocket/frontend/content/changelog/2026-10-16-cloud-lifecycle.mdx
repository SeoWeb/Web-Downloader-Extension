  date: "2026-10-16",

- **Disconnect your account from the extension cleanly.** Unlinking no longer
  leaves dangling state on either side.
- **Optional cloud delete:** removing a local save can now also delete its
  cloud copy, in one explicit step.
- **Cloud library capacity is checked before an upload starts**, so pushes to
  a full library fail fast instead of burning time on retries.
- **Platform-wide security hardening:** stricter ownership checks on every
  archived page and share link, rate limiting on sign-in and uploads,
  registration throttling, and shorter-lived access tokens.
