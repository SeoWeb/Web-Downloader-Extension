- **Single-file mode** — the whole page inlined into one HTML file, best for
  quick archiving and easy moving between machines.
- **Full-website mode** — follows the first level of linked pages and saves
  each one, for sets of pages that belong together.
- The extension's interface now ships in more than 40 languages, selectable
  from the panel.
