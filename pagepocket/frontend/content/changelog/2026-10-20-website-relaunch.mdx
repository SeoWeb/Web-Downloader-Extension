The pagepocket.app site relaunched around what the product actually does:

- **Documentation** — the guide section you are reading now, covering saving,
  collections, search, sharing, and troubleshooting.
- **Practical guides** — a blog starting with a full comparison of every way
  to save a web page.
- **Use-case pages** — how researchers, journalists, developers, and
  archivists put a private web archive to work.
- **Honest competitor comparisons** — feature tables sourced from public
  documentation, each with a "when the competitor is the better choice"
  section.
- **Proof you can verify** — testimonials are gone; what remains is the open
  [GitHub repository](https://github.com/SeoWeb/Web-Downloader-Extension) and
  the [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/web-page-downloader/aeojmgngnebhbjpncamiplkimkbnmpmk),
  plus real product screenshots.
