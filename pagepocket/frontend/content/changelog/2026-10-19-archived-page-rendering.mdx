A cross-cutting fix for the most reported rendering problem, plus quiet
reliability work across the platform:

- **Stylesheets, scripts, and documents now resolve on archived pages.** An
  asset-addressing overhaul across the extension, the cloud pipeline, and the
  viewer fixed saved pages coming up unstyled or missing files.
- **Archived files are served behind signed, per-view tokens** — archive
  content responds only to you and to the share links you created.
- **Revoking a share link now takes effect immediately**, and view counts
  count a visitor once instead of once per refresh.
- **New saves become searchable immediately**, with automatic retry if
  indexing is ever interrupted.
- **Sessions last 30 days** — you stay signed in instead of re-authenticating
  daily.
- **Quota errors name the exact limit** ("monthly page limit reached" vs
  "storage limit reached") instead of a generic failure.
