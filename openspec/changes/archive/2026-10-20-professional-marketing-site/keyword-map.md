## Notes for maintainers

- **Cluster design (D6):** the pillar targets the head term; the four
  satellite posts target commercial-intent alternatives/comparison
  terms and each links to its matching `/compare/*` page and to the
  pillar. Posts ↔ use-case pages link by persona (journalists post ↔
  `/use-cases/journalists`).
- **Registry:** individual blog post URLs are deliberately NOT in
  `MARKETING_ROUTES`; `app/sitemap.ts` appends them from `src/lib/blog.ts`.
- **Pending routes:** `/docs`, `/changelog`, `/about` are listed in
  `MARKETING_ROUTES` and ship in the parallel docs batch — keyword rows
  to be added when their pages land. Docs subpages must link only
  within the docs tree until then.
- **Honesty constraint:** secondary keywords must never be answered on
  the page with claims that are not verifiable from the product or
  linked sources (spec: Content Honesty Policy).
