Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

At Web Page Downloader, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect any personal information gathered through our Web Page Downloader Chrome extension.

Information Collection and Use

Our extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from your device or browser. The extension's sole purpose is to download and save the currently active web page, along with any linked resources from the same domain, for offline viewing.

During the download process, the extension retrieves the content of the active web page, including its HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and linked resources (e.g., images, PDFs, documents). This content is processed locally on your device and is not sent to any external servers or third parties.

Data Handling

The downloaded web page and its associated resources are stored temporarily on your device during the download process. Once the download is complete, the extension packages these files into a ZIP archive, which is then saved to your default downloads location.

The extension does not store or retain any of the downloaded data beyond the completion of the download process. The downloaded ZIP file remains stored on your device until you choose to delete it manually.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or disclose any information collected by our extension to third parties.

Security

Our extension operates entirely within the context of your local device and does not transmit any data over the internet. As such, we do not implement any specific security measures beyond those provided by your browser and operating system.
